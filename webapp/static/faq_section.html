Guest Management

Frequently Asked Questions

Find concise answers to common questions about our PG management system. If you still need help, contact our support team.

PG (Paying Guest) accommodation is a residential setup where individuals rent a room in a house or apartment, typically sharing facilities such as the kitchen and living area. Some PGs offer meals and laundry services.

To register a guest, go to the Guests section, click "Add Guest", fill in the required fields, and submit. Required fields typically include name, contact details, and ID information.
